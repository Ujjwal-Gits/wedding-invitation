    विशेषताIn every invitation

    Everything a Nepali wedding asks for, nothing it doesn’t.

    • Live music

      A song of your choice starts when the invitation opens. Guests can mute it any time.

    • RSVP collection

      Who is coming, to which ceremony, and how many. All in one list.

    • Bikram Sambat dates

      Each date in both calendars: माघ २९, २०८३ beside 12 February 2027.

    • English and Nepali

      One tap switches the language: Aarati & Sameer becomes आरती र समीर.

    • Share on WhatsApp

      A link with your names and photo in the preview, ready for every family group.

    • Countdown and a hidden date

      Days to the wedding, and a date guests scratch to reveal, like a sealed card.

    • Your private dashboard

      A private link for the couple: update details, add photos and read every reply.
