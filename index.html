निम्तो, nimtto noun

An invitation. In Nepal it is still carried from house to house by hand. Ours arrives on WhatsApp, opens with your song, and gives every ceremony in both calendars.

How it works कसरी

From a design you love to replies on your phone.

  1. 1

    Choose a design डिजाइन छान्नुहोस्

    Twenty-seven openings, from a carved Patan door to a palace gate. Ask us for a private preview before you decide.

  2. 2

    We set it up हामी सजाउँछौं

    Send your photos, names, families and events. We put everything in place, in English and Nepali.

  3. 3

    Share one link एउटै लिङ्क

    One link for WhatsApp, Viber and Messenger, with a preview card that shows your names.

  4. 4

    Guests reply पाहुनाको जवाफ

    Guests RSVP in a tap. Every reply waits for you in your private dashboard.
