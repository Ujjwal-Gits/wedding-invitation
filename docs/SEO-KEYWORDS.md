## How a landing page is built (src/site/landings.mjs → src/site/landing.mjs)

One data object per page. Besides the hero (`h1`, `lede`: the 40 to 60 word direct answer, marked `data-speak`),
`includes`, `answers`, `ideas`, `steps` and `faq` (plain `[question, answer]` pairs, 8 to 10 per page, rendered by
the shared FAQ partial with its FAQPage JSON-LD), a page can carry:

- `guide`: the long-form sections, each one H2 with an `id` (the table of contents links to it). A section takes
  `lead`, `p`, `list`, `ol`, `check` (a checklist), `sub` (H3s), `table` ({ cap, head, rows }: an HTML table with a
  caption), `wording` (copy-ready samples, `ne` in Devanagari and/or `en`), `fig` (a design still or an international
  artwork with alt text) and `note`. Inline marks: `[words](/path/)` internal link, `[words](https://...)` a cited
  source (opens in a new tab), `**bold**`, `{ne:देवनागरी}` (set as lang="ne").
- `howTitle` / `howName`: the steps' H2 and the HowTo JSON-LD name. `priceTitle` / `priceLead`: the price section.
- `about`: the page's main entities (for this map; not rendered).
- `updated`: the day the facts were checked (default `CONTENT_CHECKED`), shown as "Updated ..." and sent as
  `dateModified` on the WebPage JSON-LD.
- `relatedPosts`: `[[title, path], ...]` of blog guides on the same occasion. Empty by default; the page shows a
  "Guides" block only when the blog fills it.

## Research behind the copy (October 2026)

- Facts that sources disagree on are written as ranges, with "families differ" and a cited source, never as one rule:
  the age of bratabandha (about 5 to 13, 8 often typical), gunyo cholo (about 7 to 9), the pasni month (5th for a
  girl, 6th or 7th for a boy), the later janku ages (Chandraratha and Mahadivyaratha differ by source), the order of the
  wedding rites (it depends on the priest), and US send times (Emily Post, The Knot, Zola and Paperless Post differ
  by a few weeks).
- Not found in any source, so not claimed: how many weeks ahead Nepali families send cards (the pages say "we
  suggest"), hand-delivery customs, the meaning of duragaman in hill weddings (removed from the wedding page), any
  survey of WhatsApp invitations in Nepal, guest-count statistics. No ratings, reviews or customer numbers anywhere.
- Nepali sample wordings follow the patterns on Nepali wedding, bratabandha and pasni cards; samples we wrote
  ourselves are labelled as our own. Every BS date in a sample was converted with nepali-date-converter, and the
  weekday matches (the hero samples' weekdays were wrong before and are fixed).
- Competitors seen: bihekocard.com (free Nepali card editor with RSVP and many occasion and wording pages; the
  strongest Nepal SEO rival), nimantrana.online, anepali.com, Etsy template sellers; in the USA Joy (free digital
  invitations), Paperless Post (pay per guest), Evite (free with ads, premium per event), Greenvelope (flat per
  mailing), Punchbowl (membership), MotionStamp (animated video + RSVP page, the closest product). The USA page
  describes these pricing models without naming anyone.
- Brand risk to watch: nimto.io ("Nimto | Create Beautiful Digital Event Invitations", RSVP, guest lists) is one
  letter from Nimtto and targets "Nepali invitations".
