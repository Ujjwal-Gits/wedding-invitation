# The next 10 themes: design briefs

Status: brief only, no code. Written 2026-10-18 for the owner's request: more themes with a Hindu / Indian touch,
very premium. That means peacocks, doves, birds, butterflies and swans on water; a real stamp or seal opening;
3D, horizontal and "deeper and deeper" scrolling; and real wedding flowers and decor. They must not be copies of
Canva templates, and every body must be different.

Names checked against every catalog name in `server/lib/catalog.js`: Agni, Amalfi, Andalus, Bagaicha, Blush Roses,
Chitra, Dhaka, Dhoka, Durbar, Garden Party, Ghar, Gold Arch, Hariyo, Himal, Jali, Janti, Jhyal, Kham, Laali,
Letterpress, Mahal, Mala, Mandap, Mangal, Mehendi, Nimantrana, Noor, Olive Grove, Pardaa, Phoolbari (gurans),
Rajdwar, Rangoli, Rose Arch, Sayapatri, Shree, Sindoor, Swayambar, Tuscany, Wax Seal, Wildflower. I also checked them
against the rejected drafts (Mayur, Hansa, Kapot, Titali, Doli, Jharokha, Kalash, Mehndi, Deep, Phool Chadar).
Display fonts were picked from faces the repo does not load yet, wherever that was possible.

## Research findings

**What reads premium in 2025-2026**
- *Restraint and storytelling over excess.* WeddingSutra's 2026 forecast says luxury "is no longer about excess",
  and that each event "becomes a chapter that guests experience" ([WeddingSutra 2026](https://weddingsutra.com/blog/the-2026-wedding-tren),
  [Catersource](https://www.catersource.com/weddings/the-rise-of-experiential-luxury-weddings-why-2026-is-about-immersive-celebration)).
- *One artist's hand, and a named Indian art form.* India's award-winning e-invite studio Turmeric Ink sells
  paper pop-up arches ("Mehraab"), Rajasthani-miniature baraat parallax, a Mughal Shalimar Bagh garden and Kalamkari
  florals ([WeddingSutra on Turmeric Ink](https://weddingsutra.com/planning/looking-for-wedding-invitation-inspiration-turmeric-inks-innovative-digital-invites-will-leave-your-guests-in-awe)).
  Other studios, such as [Posh Invites](https://weddingsutra.com/vendor/posh-invites/), also mix traditional patterns
  with modern type.
- *Jewel tones, foil, emboss and textured stock.* These cover emerald, sapphire, ruby and Mughal motifs
  ([IndianWeddingCards 2025](https://indianweddingcards.com/blog/indian-wedding-card-trends-2025),
  [StationeryHQ](https://www.stationeryhq.com/pages/top-10-wedding-invitation-trends-for-2025)). Wax seals carry
  monograms or crests, and envelopes get patterned liners and vintage stamps
  ([The Knot 2025](https://theknot.com/content/wedding-invitation-trends)). One digital studio puts a wax-seal envelope
  opening on 90% of its invites ([The Digital Yes](https://www.thedigitalyes.com/blog/animated-wedding-invitation)),
  so a plain wax-seal opening is now generic. Our seals must be Indian materials: lac (लाख), brass mohar, mauli thread
  and a kumkum thumb-tika.

**What reads cheap (avoid):** clip-art Ganesh, Great Vibes on everything, gold gradient glitter, stock mandala
overlays, too many fonts and clutter ([BollywoodShaadis](https://www.bollywoodshaadis.com/articles/10-wedding-invitation-card-mistakes-every-couple-should-avoid-3776),
[DesignWizard](https://designwizard.com/blog/how-to-design-indian-wedding-invitation-card/)). Add our own rules: glossy
CGI render looks, mirrored flower copies, text over flowers, and grey, dusty or white flower heaps (they look like a funeral).

**Real wedding flowers and decor**
- *By ceremony.* The mandap traditionally uses marigold, rose and lotus. Modern mandaps add orchids, hydrangeas and
  lilies, and jasmine and chrysanthemum garlands run through both. Haldi has marigold flower curtains, petal paths and
  a flower swing. Mehndi has marigold, rose and tuberose strands, bougainvillea, petal rangoli and flower jewellery.
  The jaimala is marigold, rose and jasmine with gold thread ([Florists' Review guide](https://floristsreview.com/a-florists-guide-to-indian-weddings/)).
- *Trends.* Marigold mandaps are back in luxe forms: sheer drapes, bamboo, transparent pillars, floating
  ([GRT/Femina](https://www.grtjewels.com/grtbrides/marigold-mandaps-are-back/)). Also mogra-string ceilings,
  floating floral domes, and temple-carved mandaps with brass bells ([VenueLook 2026](https://www.venuelook.com/blog/top-wedding-decor-trends-in-india-for-2026-you-need-to-know)).
- *Torans.* Mango leaves and marigold; peepal leaves in some regions; metal or fabric leaves
  ([Wikipedia: Toran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toran_(art))). South Indian work is structured and geometric, with
  lotuses floating in brass urns ([Manyavar](https://www.manyavar.com/en-in/blogs/south-indian-wedding-decoration-ideas-marigolds-lighting.html)).
  Lotus with mogra cascades is another style ([WeddingBazaar](https://www.weddingbazaar.com/blog/lotus-mogra-wedding-decor-ideas)).
- *Nepali.* Brass kalash, diyo, supari for the Newar bride
  ([Full Time Explorer](https://fulltimeexplorer.com/nepali-wedding-traditions/)), plus sayapatri, makhamali
  (globe amaranth), godavari (chrysanthemum), gurans, dubo grass and dahi-sagun. Our shared kit already has these.
- *Rule used below.* Fresh living blooms with crisp leaves. Marigold, red and rose-pink roses, lotus, orchids and
  makhamali carry the colour. Rajnigandha and mogra appear only as fine strings or accents.

**Bird and art meanings (so the motifs are not decoration only)**
- *Peacock.* Krishna's presence, love, the monsoon and royalty ([Narodna galerija](https://www.ng-slo.si/si/qr/zd2019346),
  [Saffronart](https://saffronart.com/customauctions/PostWork.aspx?l=19869)).
- *Hamsa (swan).* Saraswati's vahana and discernment. In the Nala-Damayanti story a golden swan carries love messages
  between the two before they meet ([Wikipedia: Hamsa](https://en.wikipedia.com/wiki/Hamsa_bird),
  [Collegedunia](https://questions.collegedunia.com/exams/questions/who-is-the-woman-depicted-in-this-painting-by-raja-68edef97e62c9b3c0274f87e)).
- *Pichwai.* Nathdwara temple cloths of cows, lotuses and peacocks, with raised gold work
  ([MeMeraki](https://www.memeraki.com/collections/pichwai/products/cow-and-lotus-in-pichwai-by-jayesh-sharma),
  [Vikaspedia](https://en.vikaspedia.in/viewcontent/education/art-culture/pichwai-paintings?lgn=en)).
  Devotional, so the Ganesh and Krishna imagery stays off the international site.

**Scroll and motion techniques**
- *GSAP ScrollTrigger.* Pin, scrub, `containerAnimation` for horizontal sections, and timeline labels per scene,
  with transform and opacity only ([GSAP guide](https://dev.to/vishwark/mastering-gsap-scrolltrigger-a-complete-practical-guide-5bi3)).
- *"Deeper and deeper".* A layered zoom (Telescope-style) with ScrollSmoother
  ([Codrops, Oct 2025](https://tympanus.net/codrops/2025/10/29/building-a-layered-zoom-scroll-effect-with-gsap-scrollsmoother-and-scrolltrigger/),
  [Awwwards zoom elements](https://www.awwwards.com/inspiration/zoom-scroll-intro-telescope)).
- *Water.* Heightfield ripples ([Evan Wallace WebGL Water](https://people.math.harvard.edu/~knill/pedagogy/techdemo4/water/index.html)).
- *Alpha video for wings.* An HEVC-alpha source listed before VP9 WebM, plain `<video>` only, never as a WebGL texture,
  because Safari drops the alpha ([Jake Archibald](https://jakearchibald.com/2024/video-with-transparency/)).
- *CSS scroll-driven animations.* Safe as a progressive layer. Chrome has had them since 115; Safari arrived later
  ([Chrome blog](https://developer.chrome.com/blog/scroll-triggered-animations)).

**Production rules for every theme below**
1. Animate transform and opacity only. One signature moment per page, and everything else stays quiet. Durations of
   150-400 ms for UI with a custom ease. No parallax on mobile; there, a depth move becomes a single scale or
   crossfade per scene. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` and `?still`.
2. **Birds and butterflies are rigged, not generated as animations.** Each creature is three to five PNG parts
   (body, near wing, far wing, and the neck or tail where needed). The wings rotate about the shoulder pivot, and a
   butterfly flaps by `scaleX` on each wing. Canva cannot make consistent frame sequences, so rigging is the
   reliable route. Optional 2-3 s alpha video loops only where noted.
3. **Canva workflow.** Canva AI gives no transparency, so every "transparent PNG" asset below is generated on a flat
   plain background in the colour named, then run through Background Remover. Export at full size by the art-board
   route (see memory: canva-art-export-workflow). Panoramas are built as matching 16:9 panels joined with Magic Expand.
4. **Shared prompt suffix `[NEG]`.** Add it to every prompt:
   *"no text, no letters, no watermark, no frame or border, no glossy CGI or plastic 3D look, no oversaturated colours,
   no mirrored or copy-pasted flowers, no wilted, grey, dusty or white-heaped flowers; fresh living blooms with crisp
   green leaves, natural petal variation, soft daylight, fine-art quality."*
5. **Copy placement.** Text sits on paper, cloth, stone or sky, never over flowers. Nepali digits need `locl` off
   (see `_suite/nepali.css`). The Devanagari and B.S. dates follow the existing kit.
6. **Engine.** The Nepali and Hindu themes run on `_suite` (data, RSVP, countdown, scratch, lightbox, music). The
   np + intl themes hide the Ganesh and religious marks on the international site (no religion labels).

## 1. Kalapi (कलापी) · the peacock's monsoon

**Meaning:** Sanskrit *kalāpin*, "the one with the jewelled tail", the peacock that dances when the first rain clouds come.
**Pitch:** a Rajput-miniature monsoon evening painted on one long pichwai cloth. The guest walks along it sideways.

**Palette:** monsoon teal `#0F3D41` (ground) · malachite `#1E6A5A` · cloth ivory `#F3EAD6` (text surface) ·
kohl ink `#15201F` · antique gold `#C69A3E` (only accent: rules, buttons) · lotus pink `#D9718A` (art only).
**Type:** Cormorant Upright 500/600 (Latin display) · Jaini Purva (Devanagari display, Jain-manuscript hand) ·
Mukta (body) · Imperial Script (names only).

**Opening: the peacock fans its tail**
1. A dark teal screen shows one peacock seen from behind, its train folded and trailing.
2. Tap, and 21 feather PNGs rotate out from the tail root on a stagger (40 ms), forming the full fan.
3. The fan's eye-circle centre is plain teal cloth, and the names write in there in gold.
4. The fan lifts and scales down into the top border of the cloth, and the bird walks out of frame.

**Signature scroll: the pinned horizontal pichwai.** The section pins for 5 screen-widths and vertical scroll drives
`x` (GSAP `containerAnimation`). The cloth has five panels:
1. palace roof under monsoon clouds, with dancing peacocks;
2. lotus pond with cows and the couple photo in a scalloped cusped arch;
3. the ceremonies as Ragamala-style bordered miniatures, each on its own ivory cartouche;
4. a full moon holding the countdown;
5. a rain-cloud band.

Three feather layers move at 0.9x, 1x and 1.1x on desktop only.

**Body after the cloth (vertical):** facing family portraits as two red-bordered miniature panels; a "monsoon diary"
of photos tipped onto cloth; the scratch card as a peacock-feather eye; RSVP on a cloth cartouche.

**Flowers and decor:** pink lotus, marigold torans on the painted palace, kadamba trees, mango-leaf toran on the
roof, brass diyas along the parapet.

**Canva assets**
- `kalapi-pano-1..5`. Full-bleed, 16:9 each, one continuous horizon. Prompt: *"Rajasthani pichwai painting on
  starched cotton, deep teal monsoon night, palace terrace with peacocks dancing, rain clouds with gold edges, lotus
  pond below, mineral pigments, raised gold detailing, flat miniature perspective, panel N of a continuous frieze with
  the horizon at 62% height"* + [NEG].
- `feather` ×1. Transparent PNG, 1:4. *"single peacock tail feather, photographic, iridescent eye, on flat white"* + [NEG].
- `peacock-back`. Transparent PNG, 3:4. *"peacock seen from behind, train folded, Rajput miniature style"* + [NEG].
- `cartouche`. Transparent PNG, 3:2. *"plain ivory cloth cartouche with a thin red-and-gold miniature border, empty
  centre"* + [NEG].

**Reduced motion:** the fan is shown already open. The panorama becomes five stacked panels that crossfade in.
**Risks:** keeping the panels joined without seams; Krishna imagery must stay off the international site; the
horizontal pin needs a short end on phones (3 widths).

## 2. Sarovar (सरोवर) · the swan lake at dawn

**Meaning:** a sacred lake, the home of the hamsa, the swan that carried love letters in Nala and Damayanti.
**Pitch:** the guest glides over a living lotus lake at dawn toward a white marble chhatri, with a swan pair alongside.

**Palette:** dawn pearl `#FBF6EF` · lake jade `#2E6E70` · deep water `#12343A` · ink `#1D2A2B` ·
lotus rose `#C94F6D` (only accent) · sun gold `#E6B85C` (art only).
**Type:** Bona Nova (display, italic for names) · Tiro Devanagari Hindi · Mukta (body).

**Opening: the lotus bud**
1. One closed pink lotus bud sits on still water.
2. Tap, and a ripple ring spreads across the lake (WebGL heightfield impulse).
3. Eight petal PNGs rotate open about the base.
4. Two swans glide in from the left and right edges. Their necks bow until the heads meet, making the heart.
5. The names rise on the plain sky above the bloom, never on it.

**Signature scroll: the glide.** A fixed WebGL lake (the painted lake texture with a ripple displacement shader)
fills the lower 55% of the view. Scroll moves the camera forward. Objects stand on a perspective ground plane and
travel toward the viewer with scale and `translateY`: lotus clusters, floating diyas on leaf boats, stone ghat posts
carrying each event plaque, and the swans keeping pace. The chhatri grows from a dot to full screen at the end.

**Body:** couple photos as reflections (the photo plus its flipped, blurred twin in the water); events on stone
plaques rising out of the water at different depths; a family pavilion of two marble columns; the countdown on a
floating diya ring; RSVP by releasing a diya (yes floats out).

**Flowers and decor:** pink and white lotus with lily pads, marigold strings on the chhatri, mogra strings as fine
accents, brass lotus urns on the ghat.

**Canva assets**
- `lake-dawn`. Full-bleed, 9:16 and 16:9. *"Mughal-Rajput marble chhatri in the middle of a calm lotus lake at
  sunrise, pink lotuses and lily pads, soft mist, watercolour and gouache on handmade paper, gentle warm light"* + [NEG].
- `swan-body` / `swan-neck` / `swan-wing`. Transparent PNG, 4:3. *"elegant white mute swan side view, painterly
  realism, body only / neck only, on flat teal"* + [NEG].
- `lotus-bud`, `lotus-petal` ×3 variants, `lily-cluster` ×3. Transparent PNG, 1:1.
- `diya-boat`. Transparent PNG, 1:1. *"clay diya on a banana-leaf boat with marigold, lit"* + [NEG].
- `ghat-plaque`. Transparent PNG, 2:3. *"weathered sandstone post with a smooth empty plaque face"* + [NEG].

**Reduced motion:** a still lake without the shader; objects fade in place; the swans are a static pair.
**Risks:** WebGL cost on mid Androids (cap DPR at 1.5; CSS fallback); swans must not look like stock clip art.

## 3. Sandesh (सन्देश) · doves bring the news

**Meaning:** "the message". In the old way, birds carried word of a wedding.
**Pitch:** an old-city rooftop morning; a pair of doves delivers each part of the invitation as a hand-stamped postcard.

**Palette:** morning sky `#E7EEF0` · terrace ivory `#FFFBF3` · lac vermilion `#B3261E` (only accent: seals,
postmarks, buttons) · ink `#2A1E1A` · brass `#A57F37` · indigo wash `#3C557A` (art only).
**Type:** Old Standard TT (display) · Rhodium Libre (Devanagari display) · Martel Sans (body) · Courier Prime (postmark text only).

**Opening: the lac-sealed patra**
1. Two doves fly in (rigged wings) carrying a folded letter tied with red-and-yellow mauli thread.
2. They drop it into the centre and perch on a rooftop rail.
3. Tap the lac seal: it cracks into two halves (two PNGs rotate apart), and the thread falls.
4. The letter unfolds in three panels (rotateX hinges) to the names, with a postmark ring stamped in vermilion
   (scale 1.15 to 1, opacity in) showing the date.

**Signature scroll: the flight path.** One scroll-scrubbed path for the dove pair runs down the whole page
(MotionPath, transform only). At each section the doves swoop in and set down a postcard. The card drops with a
slight rotation and a soft shadow. Elsewhere the birds stay perched and still.

**Body:** postcards laid in a zig-zag down a rooftop wall. Each card carries its own illustrated postage stamp
(haldi, mehndi, wedding, reception) and a postmark cancel holding the date and time. The family card is two
stamps side by side. The gallery is a pinned postcard rack of photo cards. The countdown is a stamp's perforated
edge counting down. RSVP is a reply postcard dropped into a red pillar post box, and a dove takes it away.

**Flowers and decor:** a marigold and mango-leaf toran on the rooftop door, potted tulsi, red and pink rose
bunches in brass lota vases on the parapet, bougainvillea over the wall.

**Canva assets**
- `rooftop-morning`. Full-bleed, 9:16 and 16:9. *"old Indian city rooftop at morning, lime-washed walls, carved
  doorway with fresh marigold toran, bougainvillea, brass lotas with red roses, soft blue sky, gouache illustration"* + [NEG].
- `dove-body` / `dove-wing-up` / `dove-wing-down`. Transparent PNG, 1:1. *"white rock dove in flight, painterly
  realism, wings separate, on flat sky blue"* + [NEG].
- `stamp-haldi|mehndi|wedding|reception`. Transparent PNG, 4:5. *"vintage Indian postage stamp illustration of
  [marigold haldi bowl / henna cone with jasmine / mandap with marigold / lamps at night], engraved style, perforated
  edge, blank value area"* + [NEG]. Typeset the value and text in code.
- `postbox`. Transparent PNG, 1:2. *"red cast-iron pillar post box"* + [NEG].

**Reduced motion:** the doves sit perched; postcards are already placed; the postmark is static.
**Risks:** a long MotionPath on low-end phones (shorten it on mobile); rigged doves need a careful pivot; avoid
copying a real postal-service logo.

## 4. Vasant (वसन्त) · the spring garden of butterflies

**Meaning:** spring, the season of marriages and of Vasant Panchami's yellow.
**Pitch:** a botanical book come alive. For every ceremony its own flower opens, and butterflies come to it.

**Palette:** haldi cream `#FFF7E6` · turmeric `#F0B82D` (backgrounds only) · leaf green `#3B6B35` ·
rani pink `#C2185B` (only accent) · ink `#2A2415` · pale sage `#E6EEDC`.
**Type:** Ibarra Real Nova (display) · Halant (Devanagari) · Hind (body) · Italianno (names only).

**Opening: the flower basket**
1. A woven bamboo daliya covered with a fresh banana leaf.
2. Tap, and the leaf slides off with a small rotation.
3. A dozen rigged butterflies lift out on a stagger and scatter to the edges.
4. Under them, the basket is full of marigold, roses and jasmine. The names sit on the cream paper above it.

**Signature scroll: the sticky bloom window.** On desktop the screen is split. The left half is sticky and shows a
scroll-scrubbed image sequence of a flower opening (24 frames, `<canvas>` drawImage, opacity crossfade between
frames). Each ceremony has its own flower: haldi marigold, mehndi mogra and bougainvillea, wedding red rose, reception
orchid. As a flower finishes opening, two butterflies land on it. The right column scrolls the event cards on plain
paper. On mobile, a sticky 42vh bloom band sits above the text.

**Body:** the bloom sequence; a herbarium gallery (photos tipped onto botanical plates with photo corners and the
flower beside the photo, not under text); a family plate of two pressed sprays; the countdown as a butterfly
wing-count; the scratch card as a wing; RSVP as a seed packet.

**Flowers and decor:** marigold, red rose, blush rose, mogra (small), purple dendrobium orchid, bougainvillea,
makhamali, mustard flowers.

**Canva assets**
- `bloom-[flower]-01..24`. Not reliable in Canva, so generate 4 stages per flower (bud, quarter, half, full) and
  tween between them with crossfade and a slight scale. Full-bleed, 3:4. *"botanical gouache plate of a single
  [marigold] at stage [bud], cream paper, centred, leaves crisp"* + [NEG].
- `butterfly-[common-mormon|lime|crimson-rose]-wingL/wingR/body`. Transparent PNG, 1:1. *"Indian [lime] butterfly
  top view, scientific illustration accuracy, wings separate"* + [NEG].
- `daliya-basket` and `banana-leaf`. Transparent PNG, 4:3.

**Reduced motion:** each flower is shown fully open; butterflies rest still on the flowers.
**Risks:** stage-to-stage consistency (same angle per flower); keep the total image weight under 2.5 MB with lazy-loaded stages.

## 5. Mohar (मोहर) · the royal seal

**Meaning:** the royal seal pressed into lac. Mohar was also Nepal's silver coin, so the word means value too.
**Pitch:** a royal farman (decree) for a wedding: the family seal pressed live into red lac, then the decree unfolds panel by panel.

**Palette:** parchment `#F1E6CE` · kohl ink `#1B1512` · lac red `#8E1B1B` (only accent) · antique brass `#AE8840` ·
indigo cloth `#22304E` (the table) · ink faded `#5A4A3A`.
**Type:** Linden Hill (display, engraved) · Tiro Devanagari Sanskrit (Devanagari display) · Crimson Pro (body).

**Opening: the seal press, the owner's "proper stamp"**
1. A folded farman lies on indigo cloth, tied with red silk, a pool of molten lac at the knot.
2. Press and hold. A brass seal ring (the couple's monogram, from the initials slot) descends, and the lac
   spreads (scale on a lac-blob PNG).
3. Release. The seal lifts, leaving the impression (an opacity reveal of the embossed monogram layer).
4. One more tap and the lac cracks along a fracture line (two halves rotate apart). The silk slips away.

**Signature scroll: the concertina farman.** The decree is six accordion panels hinged in 3D. On desktop it is
pinned and unfolds horizontally (`rotateY` alternating ±, `perspective` 1600px). On mobile it unfolds vertically
(`rotateX`). Each panel is one section, headed by its own small lac seal impression: invocation, families, ceremonies,
venue, countdown, blessings.

**Body after the farman:** a red cloth-bound bahi-khata (the Indian ledger) as the RSVP. The guest's name appears as
if inked in the register, and the page turns once. Photos are a "royal portrait" gallery in brass frames on the
indigo cloth.

**Flowers and decor:** kept off the decree. Marigold and rose pankhudi (petals) scattered on the cloth table,
rajnigandha sprigs beside the seal tray, a brass paan-daan and a diya.

**Canva assets**
- `farman-panel`. Full-bleed, 2:3. *"aged handmade parchment with faint Mughal gold-sprinkled border, empty centre,
  top-lit"* + [NEG].
- `indigo-table`. Full-bleed, 16:9 and 9:16. *"indigo cotton cloth on a carved wooden table, scattered fresh
  marigold and red rose petals at the edges, brass paan-daan, warm lamplight, photographic"* + [NEG].
- `seal-ring`. Transparent PNG, 1:1. *"antique brass signet seal with a blank flat face, side view"* + [NEG].
  The monogram is set in code.
- `lac-blob`, `lac-half-L`, `lac-half-R`. Transparent PNG, 1:1. *"pool of deep red lac sealing wax, glossy, organic edge"* + [NEG].
- `bahi-khata`. Transparent PNG, 4:3. *"red cloth-bound Indian ledger tied with string, open blank pages"* + [NEG].

**Reduced motion:** the seal is already pressed and the farman fully open as a vertical stack.
**Risks:** the hold-to-press must also work as a single tap (accessibility); 3D hinge panels need
`backface-visibility` testing on iOS.

## 6. Toran (तोरण) · seven doorways

**Meaning:** the garland hung over a door to welcome guests and good fortune.
**Pitch:** the guest walks deeper and deeper through seven dressed doorways of a haveli, each with a different real
toran, to the mandap at the heart of the house.

**Palette:** lime-wash `#F7F1E2` · mango leaf `#2F5D2C` · marigold `#EC8A1C` (only accent) · deep haveli red
`#6D1F1A` · ink `#22180F` · courtyard shade `#D9CDB4`.
**Type:** Kalnia (display) · Palanquin Dark (Devanagari display) · Mukta (body).

**Opening: the phool-ladi curtain**
