The best wedding invitation wording examples share one plan: who is hosting, a line asking guests to come, your names, the date and time, the place, and how to reply. Formal wording spells everything out in words. Casual wording keeps the same order but sounds like you talking.

Below you will find the anatomy of an invitation, the formal rules most etiquette guides agree on, and 19 copy-ready examples. They cover parents hosting, the couple hosting, divorced parents, a parent who has passed, small weddings, destination trips, courthouse ceremonies and online invitations.

## The Anatomy of a Wedding Invitation

Every invitation, formal or relaxed, follows the same basic order. [The Knot](https://www.theknot.com/content/standard-wedding-invitation-wording-examples) suggests keeping the main card to the essentials and moving extra details to an enclosure card or wedding website. Here is what each line does.

| Line | What it says | Formal example | Casual example |
|---|---|---|---|
| Host line | Who is inviting | Mr. and Mrs. David Carter | Together with their families |
| Request line | The ask | request the pleasure of your company | invite you to celebrate |
| Couple | Your names | Emma Rose Carter and James Lee Park | Emma and James |
| Date | Day, date, year | Saturday, the twelfth of June, two thousand twenty-seven | Saturday, June 12, 2027 |
| Time | Start time | at half after four o'clock | at 4:30 p.m. |
| Venue | Place and city | The Glasshouse, Portland, Oregon | The Glasshouse, Portland |
| Reception line | What comes next | Dinner and dancing to follow | Party to follow! |
| RSVP | How and when to reply | The favor of a reply is requested by the fifteenth of May | Please reply by May 15 |

You can drop the host line on a casual invitation. Everything else should stay, because guests need it to plan.

## Formal Wedding Invitation Wording Rules

Formal wedding invitation wording has a few set habits. You do not have to follow all of them, but knowing them helps you break them on purpose.

### "Honor of your presence" or "pleasure of your company"

[Emily Post](https://emilypost.com/advice/formal-wedding-invitation-wording) treats "the honour of your presence" as the most solemn request and ties it to the type of ceremony setting. "The pleasure of your company" is the traditional choice for venues such as hotels, gardens, homes and event halls. Emily Post also keeps the British "u" in "honour". Many American couples write "honor", which is just as correct. Choose one spelling and stick with it.

### Dates and times in words

Formal invitations write the date, the year and the time in words. [Emily Post](https://emilypost.com/advice/formal-wedding-invitation-wording) prefers "half after four o'clock" to "half past" or "four-thirty", and leaves "and" out of the year ("two thousand twenty-seven"). [WeddingWire](https://www.weddingwire.com/wedding-ideas/wedding-invitation-wording-decoded) accepts "half past" and adds "in the afternoon" or "in the morning" where it helps. Both are fine. Just be consistent.

### When to use "and" between names

"And" tells the reader that two people are married or marrying. That is why [Minted](https://www.minted.com/wedding-ideas/wedding-invitation-wording) and WeddingWire both advise putting divorced parents on separate lines with no "and" between them. When one family hosts, many invitations join the couple with "to" ("their daughter Emma Rose to James Lee Park"). When both families or the couple host, "and" is the usual choice.

### Names and titles

Use full names on formal invitations, including middle names if you like. Emily Post abbreviates titles such as "Mr." and "Mrs." but writes out "Doctor" when it fits on the line. Keep both partners' names in the same style: two full names, or two first names, never one of each.

> [!TIP]
> Read your wording out loud before you print or publish it. If a line sounds stiff when you say it, your guests will hear it that way too.

## Formal and Traditional Wording Examples

These examples follow formal wedding invitation wording. Swap in your own names, date and venue.

```copy Formal invitation from the bride's parents
Mr. and Mrs. David Carter
request the pleasure of your company
at the marriage of their daughter
Emma Rose
to
James Lee Park
Saturday, the twelfth of June
two thousand twenty-seven
at half after four o'clock
The Glasshouse
Portland, Oregon
Dinner and dancing to follow
```

```copy Formal invitation from both sets of parents
Mr. and Mrs. David Carter
and
Mr. and Mrs. Daniel Park
request the pleasure of your company
at the marriage of their children
Emma Rose Carter
and
James Lee Park
Saturday, the twelfth of June
two thousand twenty-seven
at half after four o'clock
The Glasshouse
Portland, Oregon
```

### Divorced parents wedding invitation wording

Each parent gets their own line, with no "and" between them. A stepparent shares a line with the parent they are married to, as [WeddingWire](https://www.weddingwire.com/wedding-ideas/wedding-invitation-wording-decoded) explains. Minted notes that the mother is usually listed first.

```copy Divorced and remarried parents hosting
Mrs. Linda Moore
Mr. and Mrs. David Carter
request the pleasure of your company
at the marriage of their daughter
Emma Rose Carter
to
James Lee Park
Saturday, the twelfth of June
two thousand twenty-seven
at four o'clock in the afternoon
The Glasshouse
Portland, Oregon
```

### A parent who has passed

Etiquette guides agree that a parent who has died cannot be named as a host. You can still name them gently beside their child's name, using "the late" as WeddingWire suggests, or simply "daughter of".

```copy Honoring a parent who has passed
Mrs. Linda Carter
requests the pleasure of your company
at the marriage of her daughter
Emma Rose Carter
daughter of the late David Carter
to
James Lee Park
Saturday, the twelfth of June
two thousand twenty-seven
at half after four o'clock
The Glasshouse
Portland, Oregon
```

> [!NOTE]
> If naming a parent on the invitation feels too heavy, honor them another way: a line on your invitation website, a reserved seat, or a quiet moment in the speeches. The invitation itself can simply say "Together with their families".

## Wedding Invitation Wording From the Bride and Groom

More couples host their own weddings now. In that case you can open with your own names, or with a shared line. WeddingWire suggests "Together with their families" when the couple and parents host together.

```copy From the couple, formal
Emma Rose Carter
and
James Lee Park
request the pleasure of your company
at their marriage
Saturday, the twelfth of June
two thousand twenty-seven
at half after four o'clock
The Glasshouse
Portland, Oregon
```

```copy From the couple, with their families
Together with their families
Emma Carter and James Park
invite you to celebrate their wedding
Saturday, June 12, 2027
at 4:30 p.m.
The Glasshouse, Portland, Oregon
Dinner and dancing to follow
```

### Same-sex couple

The wording does not need to change at all. For name order, [Minted](https://www.minted.com/wedding-ideas/wedding-invitation-wording) suggests alphabetical order or whatever looks best in the design.

```copy Same-sex couple
Together with their families
Daniel Brooks
and
Marcus Reyes
invite you to celebrate their marriage
Saturday, the fourth of September
two thousand twenty-seven
at five o'clock in the evening
Willow Creek Farm
Asheville, North Carolina
```

### Second wedding or vow renewal

For a second marriage, the couple usually hosts, and the tone can be warm and grown-up. A vow renewal says "renewal of vows" or "celebrate ten years" so no one thinks it is a first wedding.

```copy Second wedding
Laura Bennett and Michael Grant
joyfully invite you to their wedding
Saturday, October 16, 2027
at 3:00 p.m.
The Garden Room, Napa, California
A family dinner will follow
```

```copy Vow renewal
Ten years, two kids and one very old dog later
Sara and Tom Alvarez
invite you to celebrate the renewal of their vows
Saturday, August 21, 2027
at 6:00 p.m.
Harbor House, Seattle, Washington
Dinner and dancing to follow
```

## Casual and Modern Wedding Invitation Wording

Casual wedding invitation wording keeps the same facts but drops the formal phrases. [Emily Post](https://emilypost.com/advice/informal-wedding-invitation-wording) notes that less formal couples can write naturally instead of using the set lines.

```copy Casual
We're getting married!
Emma and James
would love for you to join us
Saturday, June 12, 2027, at 4:30 p.m.
The Glasshouse, Portland
Food, drinks and dancing to follow
```

```copy Modern and minimal
Emma + James
June 12, 2027
4:30 p.m. ceremony
6:00 p.m. dinner and dancing
The Glasshouse, Portland
Come celebrate with us
```

```copy Small and intimate
We're keeping it small, and we want you there.
Emma and James
invite you to their wedding
Saturday, June 12, 2027, at 1:00 p.m.
Lunch to follow at Juniper Kitchen, Portland
```

### Adults-only wedding

[Zola](https://www.zola.com/expert-advice/how-to-say-no-kids-in-the-wedding-invitation) says the clearest signal is addressing the invitation only to the people invited, and warns that a bare "adults only" can read as harsher than you mean. A kind line works better.

```copy Adults-only line
We love your little ones, but our celebration
will be for adults only.
Thank you for understanding, and enjoy a night off!
```

## Wording for Special Situations

### Destination wedding

Give guests the place and dates up front, because they will be booking travel.

```copy Destination wedding
Pack your bags!
Emma Carter and James Park
invite you to join them for their wedding
Saturday, June 12, 2027
Villa Rosa, Positano, Italy
Travel and hotel details are on our wedding website
Please reply by March 15, 2027
```

### Courthouse wedding with a party later

[Zola](https://www.zola.com/expert-advice/a-guide-to-reception-only-invitations) advises making it clear that you are already married, so guests know they are coming to a celebration, not a ceremony.

```copy Courthouse wedding, party later
We said "I do" at City Hall!
Now we want to celebrate with the people we love.
Emma and James Park
invite you to their wedding party
Saturday, August 7, 2027, at 7:00 p.m.
The Loft, Portland, Oregon
Dinner, drinks and dancing
```

### Reception-only invitation

[The Knot](https://www.theknot.com/content/standard-wedding-invitation-wording-examples) suggests a small change to the request line, such as "to celebrate the wedding of", when guests are invited to the reception only.

```copy Reception only
Mr. and Mrs. David Carter
request the pleasure of your company
at a reception to celebrate the marriage of
Emma Rose Carter and James Lee Park
Saturday, the twelfth of June
two thousand twenty-seven
at seven o'clock in the evening
The Glasshouse
Portland, Oregon
```

### Multi-day wedding weekend

When every guest is invited to every event, [Zola](https://www.zola.com/expert-advice/wedding-weekend-events-what-to-include-in-your-main-invitation-vs-enclosure) recommends an itinerary with each event's date, time, place and dress. Keep each entry short.

```copy Wedding weekend itinerary
We would love for you to join us all weekend.

Friday, June 11
Welcome dinner, 7:00 p.m.
Rooftop at The Marlow, Portland
Smart casual

Saturday, June 12
Ceremony, 4:30 p.m., followed by dinner and dancing
The Glasshouse, Portland
Formal

Sunday, June 13
Farewell brunch, 10:30 a.m.
Garden Cafe, Portland
Come as you are
```

If some guests are invited to only some events, send separate details so no one sees an event they are not invited to. An online invitation with an RSVP for each event, like a [wedding invitation with RSVP](/international/wedding-invitation-with-rsvp/), makes this easier to manage.

## Wording for Online Invitations and Bilingual Guests

If half your guests speak another language, a short note in that language shows care. Put it near the top, not hidden at the end.

```copy Bilingual note line
English and Spanish details are both below.
Los detalles en español están abajo.
```

When you send an online invitation, the message you write with the link matters as much as the invitation. Keep it short and personal.

```copy Message to send with an online invitation link
Hi [Guest's name]!
We're getting married on Saturday, June 12, 2027, in Portland, and we'd love for you to be there.
Here's our invitation with all the details: [link]
Please reply on the invitation by May 15.
With love, Emma and James
```

## Common Wording Mistakes to Avoid

- **Mixing styles.** Formal words with a numeric date, or one full name next to one nickname, looks unplanned.
- **Too much on the main card.** Registry, dress code essays and hotel blocks belong on a details card or website.
- **No RSVP date.** Always give a clear reply-by date. Our guide on [when to send wedding invitations](/international/blog/when-to-send-wedding-invitations/) explains how to pick it.
- **Unclear guest names.** If children or plus-ones are not invited, address the invitation clearly instead of leaving guests to guess.
- **Skipping the proofread.** Check every name spelling, the day of the week against the date, and the venue address.

## Final Thoughts

Good wedding invitation wording is clear first and pretty second. Pick the tone that fits your day, keep the order guests expect, and use the examples above as a starting point you can make your own.
