# Hariyo (हरियो) — sage-green watercolour garden

**Concept.** A calm Canva-style garden card on handmade lokta paper: watercolour sage greenery, an ivory arch with a soft
watercolour Ganesh, शुभ विवाह in Gotu, the names in Corinthia, and a white garden pavilion with the couple (bride in a
blush lehenga, groom in ivory daura and Dhaka topi) between two peacocks. Very little text per screen.

**Opening (signature motion): the leaves part.** The gate is a garden: eight copies of one greenery spray (mirrored and rotated)
grow in from the four corners, both side edges and over two shoulders of the arch. Tap anywhere, and the arch fades
while every layer slides out to its own corner or edge at its own speed (the near sprigs fastest, a small parallax),
three loose leaves drift down through the clearing, the paper clears and the pavilion rises into place. Everything after
that is quiet: one leaf drifts past now and then and the pavilion breathes (0.012 scale), both paused off screen and off
under reduced motion. Only transform and opacity animate; no filters, no permanent will-change.

**The body: a walk into the garden.** After the hero (pavilion with the couple and peacocks), the page is a pinned,
scroll-scrubbed walk forward through the garden. Each scene sits inside a trellis arch dressed in greenery (mirrored and
rotated copies of the spray, dressed differently per scene). Scroll and the arch grows past the camera and fades while the
next scene comes up out of the distance; two loose leaves fly past in the foreground, nearer than anything.
1. The couple in a living hedge arch. 2. The date: a watercolour seed packet whose leafy green cover you scratch away
(`T.scratch.paint` = `leafCover`). 3. The countdown: four carved stepping stones on a lawn, receding into the garden.
4. The families: two arbours with benches, turned to face each other.
Then the pin releases for long content: the programme as wooden signposts on a post (each arrow board swings round in
3D to face you as you scroll), photographs hanging from vines at three depths (parallax; they sway gently while in view;
you step through a greenery arch to enter), the full card as a letter with a Dhaka border, a calm RSVP, the blessing.
Phones: the same walk, every scene sized to one screen. Reduced motion: scenes stack, no pin, no sway.

**Palette / type.** Paper #FAFBF6, sage #4E6B4F (accent, AA on paper and white), olive, blush, gold hairlines #B8A06A.
Bellefair (Latin display), Corinthia (names), Gotu (Devanagari display), Mukta (body, both scripts).

**Art.** Canva AI, generated for this theme (`public/themes/hariyo/art/`): pavilion, greenery, ganesh. The greenery's
translucent (keyed-off-white) flowers were filled opaque so the sprays can overlap; `leaf-a/b/c` are small feathered
crops of the same spray, used for the drifting leaves.
