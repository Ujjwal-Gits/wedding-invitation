  2. 02

    Order in two minutes

    Make your account with your name, email and a password, or sign in. Then choose your plan and design and add your WhatsApp number. Your account brings you back any time.

    • No long forms: we ask the rest on WhatsApp
    • The price is shown before you continue
  3. 03

    Pay by QR, send a screenshot

    The payment page shows a QR for Khalti, eSewa or any bank app, with your order number. From abroad, pay by PayPal. Then upload the payment screenshot. We check it and confirm.

    • Khalti, eSewa, Fonepay bank QR and PayPal
    • Pay later? Your account brings you back to the payment page
  4. 04

    We make it, you share it

    We message you on WhatsApp for names, dates, venues, photos and your song, and show you every change. Then you send one link to everyone.

    • Guests open it in any phone browser
    • They see every event, with maps, and reply
    • You read the replies in your dashboard
