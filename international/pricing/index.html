Is a digital invitation cheaper than paper?

Usually, yes. Printed invitations, envelopes and stamps for a full guest list can cost hundreds of dollars, and changes mean printing again. A Nimtto invitation is one payment from $49, with no charge per guest, and you can change details at any time for free.

Are there hidden fees?

No. The plan price is the whole price, paid once in US dollars. There is no monthly fee, no charge per guest and no fee to change details. A custom design is quoted first: we reply within one business day with a price, and you pay only if you are happy with it.
