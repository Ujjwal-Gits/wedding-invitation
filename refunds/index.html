In a critical case, you can ask us for a refund. We look at each request and may approve a full or partial refund, or decline it. Refunds are our decision and are never automatic.

If something does not work or looks wrong, tell us and we fix it for you, at no extra cost.

A custom design starts as an enquiry: we reply with a price, and you pay only if you agree. Once paid, the same rule applies.
